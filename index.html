Meet the tonic

Sweet heat with a wish

The Allergenie's Honey Fire Tonic balances bright vinegar tang, raw honey sweetness, and a slow-building habanero glow. Every bottle is a playful nod to making wellness wishes come true.

Real ingredients

Every bottle is made with raw apple cider vinegar, local honey, and a hand-cut mix of onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, horseradish, and habanero. Nothing artificial, ever — just pure genie-level goodness.

  • Organic and gluten-free ingredients
  • Small batches for consistent flavor
  • Hand-poured and labeled in Akron, Ohio

How to enjoy

Sip a tablespoon as a morning wake-up, shake into your favorite mocktail, drizzle over roasted vegetables, or mix into marinades for a honey-hot kick. The Allergenie loves granting flavor wishes.

Refrigerate after opening to keep every pour bright and lively.

Give it a gentle shake — botanicals settle naturally in the bottle.

Start small if you're sensitive to spice; Honey Fire finishes with a warm, genie-lamp glow.
