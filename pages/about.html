About

The Allergenie & Cadence Holistic

The Allergenie is our playful, wish-granting brand for Honey Fire Tonic. We pair bright apple cider vinegar with locally sourced honey and hand-prepared botanicals, all under the Cadence Holistic umbrella led by the same CEO. It lets us bring fun, magic, and care to every bottle while honoring a broader mission of inclusive wellness.

From sourcing organic ingredients to bottling by hand, we keep the process transparent and community-centered. We regularly collaborate on workshops and tastings so you can experience the tonic's sweet heat, sparkle, and glow in person.
